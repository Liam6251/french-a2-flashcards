# 🇫🇷 Mots A2: French Flashcards

The 100 most important, most frequently used French words at A2 level, as interactive flashcards with **real native-speaker pronunciation**.

**▶ Open it:** https://liam6251.github.io/french-a2-flashcards/

## Features
- **Cards**: 100 words in 5 categories (Verbs, Nouns, Adjectives, Time & place, Little words), each with an example sentence and translation. Articles are colour-coded masculine/feminine.
- **Real audio**: every word is recorded by a native French speaker (Lingua Libre / Wikimedia Commons). Includes slow mode and auto-play.
- **Conjugation**: all 30 verbs in the **présent** and **imparfait**, with imparfait endings highlighted. Tap any line to hear it.
- **All words**: a searchable list with progress dots.
- Know it / Again tracking (saved in the browser), starred cards, filters, shuffle, FR→EN or EN→FR, type-the-answer mode
- Swipe cards on a phone; keyboard shortcuts on a computer (Space, ←/→, 1, 2, P, F, S, D, T)
- Dark and light themes

## Audio credits
Word recordings come from [Lingua Libre](https://lingualibre.org) volunteers via Wikimedia Commons, licensed CC BY-SA. The speaker and source file for each word are in [`audio/credits.json`](audio/credits.json). Example sentences and conjugations use the device's built-in French voice.
