# 🇫🇷 French A2 Flashcards

100 of the most important, most frequently used French words at A2 level, as interactive flashcards.

**▶ Open it:** https://liam6251.github.io/french-a2-flashcards/

## Features
- 100 words in 5 categories: Verbs, Nouns, Adjectives, Time & place, Little words
- Example sentence with translation on every card
- 🔊 French pronunciation (plus auto-pronounce mode)
- FR → EN or EN → FR direction
- ⌨️ Type-the-answer mode (accents optional)
- ✓ Know it / ✗ Again, with progress saved in your browser
- Filter: not known yet, marked "again", ⭐ starred
- Shuffle, dark mode, works on phone
- Keyboard: Space flip · ←/→ navigate · 1 again · 2 know · P speak · F star · S shuffle · D direction · T typing
